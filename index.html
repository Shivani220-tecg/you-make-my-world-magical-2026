DIL SE LIKHA EK KHAT

Ek baat kehni thi... ♡

Pata hai, kabhi-kabhi zindagi mein kuch log bina kisi wajah ke bahut khaas ban jaate hain.

Na unhe khaas banne ki koshish karni padti hai, na hi unhe kuch alag karna padta hai. Bas unka apna hona hi kaafi hota hai.

Tumhare saath bhi kuch aisa hi hai. Tumhari chhoti-chhoti baatein, tumhara hasi-mazaak, tumhara apna alag sa andaaz... ye sab apne aap mein bahut khaas hai.

Shayad tumhe kabhi pata bhi na chale ki tumhari ek chhoti si smile kisi ka poora din achha kar sakti hai. Kabhi tumhari koi baat yaad aakar bina wajah chehre par muskaan la sakti hai.

Main chahti hoon ki tum apni zindagi mein bahut saari khoobsurat cheezein dekho. Tumhare saare sapne poore hon. Tumhe woh har khushi mile jiske liye tumne kabhi dil se dua maangi ho.

Jab kabhi kuch achha na lage, jab lage ki cheezein tumhare hisaab se nahi ho rahi, tab bhi khud par bharosa mat khona. Har din ek jaisa nahi hota, aur har mushkil ke baad ek nayi subah aati hai.

Hamesha apni hasi sambhal kar rakhna. Apne sapne bade rakhna. Aur kabhi khud ko kam mat samajhna.

Kuch log zindagi ki kitaab mein ek khoobsurat panne ki tarah hote hain. Unhe padhkar achha lagta hai, aur unki yaad bhi apne aap mein khaas hoti hai.

Bas tum bhi aise hi rehna... apne jaise, khush, befikr aur roshan.

Aur haan, ye chhoti si duniya sirf tumhare chehre par ek smile laane ke liye hai.

Tum sach mein bahut khaas ho. ♡
